Frequently asked questions.

Got questions? We've got answers about files, proofs, and shipping.

What file formats do you accept?

We prefer high-resolution PDF, AI, or PSD files saved in CMYK color mode with at least 300 DPI resolution.

What is your standard turnaround time?

Most standard print orders are processed, printed, and ready for shipment or pickup within 3 to 5 business days.

Can I request custom paper stocks?

Yes! Reach out directly via email for specialty textures, cotton papers, or custom foil stamp orders.
